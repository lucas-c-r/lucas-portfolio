Purchase order to payment

How supplier invoices reached payment

A supplier could only be paid once a receipt had been posted against its purchase order. Every supplier payment went through this step, from office supplies to engineering services and health insurance. For example, a R$500 invoice on a PO with R$1,200 of balance left R$700 for the next payments.

01

Invoice received

A supplier invoice arrived, referencing a purchase order.

02

Check the PO balance

If the PO didn’t have enough balance, I asked the responsible team to raise a purchase requisition, so the PO could be increased or a new PO created.

03

Post the receipt

With the PO correct, I posted the receipt in MIGO for the invoiced amount, reducing the PO’s remaining balance.

MIGO
04

Hand over to Finance

I sent the invoice to Finance, who posted it in MIRO against the PO and receipt, then made the payment.

MIRO · Finance
