I’m a Brazilian data professional based in London. I hold a bachelor’s degree in Business Administration from Universidade Federal Fluminense (UFF), one of Brazil’s leading universities, and I first worked with SAP and operational data as a Supply Chain Intern at MAN Energy Solutions in Rio de Janeiro.

At Lacoste, I spent nearly two years managing footwear product data in SAP S/4HANA and keeping it consistent with the PLM. One of my favourite achievements was building two Excel macros that automated pre-upload validation and helped me identify records requiring correction.

I’m now developing my SQL and Power BI skills through personal projects. I’m also studying part-time for an MSc in Sport Management and Marketing at Birkbeck. Sport, especially football, has always been a big part of my life, and I’m interested in how data helps organisations understand their performance and make better decisions.
