Case study · Lacoste

Product data quality & process improvement at Lacoste

As a Master Data Management Executive in Lacoste’s London footwear operation, I managed product data in SAP S/4HANA and supported seasonal product readiness. My work combined material creation and maintenance, SAP–DeSL PLM reconciliation, Excel automation and coordination with teams across London, Paris and Asia.

01 · Data quality

Keeping SAP and PLM aligned

I built Excel reconciliation templates using XLOOKUP and EXACT to compare product attributes exported from SAP and PLM. These checks helped identify inconsistent values and products requiring a status review. Before making changes in SAP, I confirmed discrepancies with the responsible team.

02 · Automation

Automating pre-upload validation

I developed two Excel macros to validate product planning data and material upload templates, so problems were caught before anything reached SAP.

03 · Seasonal readiness

Preparing new and carryover products for each season

Each season, I prepared both new and carryover products in SAP.

🆕

New products

  • Created new materials in SAP S/4HANA.
  • Kept their data aligned with the DeSL PLM.
🔁

Carryover products

  • Reviewed existing products carried over to the current season.
  • Updated product status and buying-phase information.
  • Confirmed vendor assignments with the Asia team.
  • Applied pricing updates supplied by Finance.
