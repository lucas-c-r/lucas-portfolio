Keeping Lacoste’s product data right, from PLM to SAP

I joined Lacoste’s Footwear office in London in August 2024 as Master Data Management Executive. It was a fantastic period for me. I owned Lacoste’s material data in SAP S/4HANA and built the tools that kept it aligned with the PLM.

The role

What I was responsible for

Two systems had to tell the same story: the PLM, where products are designed and changed, and SAP, where they are bought, priced and sold.

🧩

Material master data

  • Created materials in SAP S/4HANA.
  • Owned all of Lacoste’s material information in SAP.
  • Made sure SAP always matched the DeSL PLM, even when product details changed in the PLM.
💷

Pricing in the buy phase

  • Priced materials during the buy phase.
  • Ran cost runs in SAP.
  • Linked each material to the correct supplier, using the finance team’s margin document.

What I built · 2

A one-click validation macro

Before uploading new materials from the PLM into SAP, every material had to pass a validation check. I wrote an Excel macro that runs the whole check at once, so a clean batch could go straight to upload.

Before
~30 minutes

A manual validation that grew with every material in the batch.

→
After
A few seconds

One click: the macro validates the full batch and confirms it is ready to upload to SAP.
